Solana Token-2022 Security Questions

What is the Solana Token-2022 PermanentDelegate risk?

The PermanentDelegate extension grants an immutable address unconditional authority to transfer or burn tokens from any holder’s Associated Token Account (ATA) without the owner’s signature — even on hardware wallets.

How can TransferFeeConfig become a 100% honeypot trap?

Mints with an active transferFeeConfigAuthority can queue a newerTransferFee up to 10,000 basis points (100%), confiscating up to 100% of tokens on every DEX sell once active.

How does a malicious Solana TransferHook block selling?

Every TransferChecked call executes the external transferHook.programId via CPI. If that program reverts on transfers into AMM pools for non-whitelisted users, buyers cannot sell.
