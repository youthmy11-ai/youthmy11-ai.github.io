3. Stereo Mid/Side Background Music & SFX Preservation

Replacing the entire audio track kills emotional pacing. By decomposing stereo audio into Mid = (L+R)/2 (center dialogue) and Side = (L-R)/2 (wide stereo music, room reverb & SFX), our dubbing mixer carves vocal frequencies out of Mid during active speech while keeping the stereo music bed audible at ~-27.1 dBFS underneath -17.1 dBFS dubbed dialogue.
