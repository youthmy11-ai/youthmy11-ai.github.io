TECHNICAL FAQ

Voiceover WPM Pacing, FFmpeg atempo & AI Media Rights Questions

How many words fit in an 8s vertical video vs a 30s commercial?

At 150 WPM, target 19–21 words for an 8.0s vertical clip and 74–76 words for a 30.0s spot to land cleanly inside the ±0.5s golden window at 1.00x natural speed.

What happens if my script requires FFmpeg atempo > 1.20x?

Above 1.20x, WSOLA time-stretching crushes natural breath pauses and creates metallic pitch/formant artifacts. Trim the recommended word count in MediaBrief Studio before synthesis.

How does the Likeness Consent flag (consent=0 vs consent=1) work?

By default (consent=0), real celebrity or politician names trigger a CRITICAL pre-flight block. Enable consent=1 only when you hold verifiable identity and signed likeness permission for that speaker/talent.
