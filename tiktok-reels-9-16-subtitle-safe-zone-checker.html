9:16 Vertical Subtitle Safe-Zone, CPS & Animated .ASS Questions

Where is the safest Y-axis position on a 1080x1920 frame?

Anchor bottom-centered captions (Alignment: 2) with MarginV: 420–430px and MarginL/R: 90px. That places 1–2 caption lines cleanly between Y = 1180px and Y = 1490px, above bottom descriptions and left of action icons.

Why keep mobile captions at <= 20 CPS and <= 32 chars/line?

Above 20 CPS, viewers cannot read captions and watch visual action simultaneously on a vertical screen. Keeping lines <= 32 characters prevents horizontal collision with TikTok/Reels right-rail buttons.

How do I burn exported .ASS captions into an MP4 with FFmpeg?
