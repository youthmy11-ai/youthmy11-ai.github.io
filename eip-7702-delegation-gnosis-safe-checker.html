EIP-7702 Delegation & Gnosis Safe Verification Questions

How do you detect an EIP-7702 delegation on an EOA?

Post-Pectra/Isthmus, an EOA with an active Type-0x04 SetCode authorization returns a 23-byte indicator from eth_getCode starting with 0xef0100 followed by the 20-byte delegated contract address.

Why are chain_id = 0 EIP-7702 signatures so risky?

An EIP-7702 authorization signed with chain_id = 0 can be replayed across every EIP-7702-compatible EVM chain, allowing malicious sweepers to hijack the EOA simultaneously on Ethereum, Base, Arbitrum, BSC, and Polygon.

How does Gnosis Safe M-of-N verification work?

WalletLens calls getThreshold() (0xe75235b8) and getOwners() (0xa0e67e2b) live on each selected EVM network so you can confirm multi-signer quorums and spot L1/L2 signer drift before sending funds.
