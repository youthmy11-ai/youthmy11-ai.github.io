FAQ

How zero-backend delivery & verification works

How does instant BSC USDT auto-verification work?

Your browser queries public BNB Smart Chain JSON-RPC nodes using eth_getTransactionReceipt, validates the official BSC USDT token contract and ERC-20 Transfer log to our public address, and immediately derives your checksummed Pro key.

What if I pay with PayPal, TRC20, or Binance Pay?

Each checkout tab generates a pre-filled receipt email button to youthmy11@gmail.com. Once confirmed, you receive your Pro license key and package download link.

Are CryptoBill invoices stored on any server?

No. Everything runs locally in your browser. Shareable client links encode the invoice payload cleanly inside the URL fragment (#inv=...) so no invoice data is ever uploaded to a third-party backend.

Are the QR codes generated via an external API?

No. Every payment QR code across the storefront and CryptoBill invoices is rendered deterministically offline using our pure ES module QR Version 1–10 generator (assets/qr.js).
